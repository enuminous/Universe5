02 / Guided learning

From random spins to testable claims

Five-minute tutorial

1. Select Isotropic null, keep the seed, and run. Random spin vectors should yield mean |spin·z| near 0.5, with fluctuations.
2. Select Positive control, reset and run. The deliberately preferred z direction should push the mean alignment toward 1. This validates the diagnostic, not cosmic rotation.
3. Select Zero-spin negative control. The mean alignment becomes zero by convention; there is no oriented spin axis.
4. Increase G or reduce ε. Watch the trajectory and numerical-energy error, which may grow for close encounters.
5. Export JSON to preserve the seed, parameters, and measured residuals for comparison.

What's in the mathematics?

Gravity: pairwise softened Newtonian attraction, with a conserved numerical Hamiltonian E = Σ½m|v|² − GΣmᵢmⱼ/√(rᵢⱼ²+ε²).

Rotation: unit-quaternion updates stand in for Spin(3) / Clifford rotors. An axial generator satisfies J² = −I on its transverse plane, but that identity does not force nonzero spin.

Light/scalar: a displayed sample φ(t)=A cos(ωt) is an uncoupled indicator. Real light pressure, photon momentum exchange, and the EFMW scalar source require additional field equations.

Recursion: the event map X(0⁺)=ℛ[X(0⁻)] remains a research hypothesis. This simulation does not secretly impose or simulate a rebound.
